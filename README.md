# Ex.No.-1.1-Design and Draft the given 2D Sketches in modelling software.

## AIM

To sketch the given drawing with dimensions using fusion 360 tool.

## REQUIREMENTS

  1. Autodesk fusion 360
  2. Windows 10
  3. 16 GB of RAM (integrated graphics recommend 2 GB or more)
  4. 2.5 Mbps or faster download; 500 Kbps or faster upload 
  
## PROCEDURE
1. Create a sketch: Start a new 2D sketch on the XY plane centered at the origin.
 
2. Draw the center hub: Sketch a large outer base circle and a smaller inner hole circle at the origin.
 
3. Create the center splines: Draw one small semi-circular cutout on the inner circle and use a Circular Pattern (6 instances) to complete the gear-like center.

4. Place the top ear: Draw two concentric circles directly above the origin at your target vertical distance.

6. Place the bottom ears: Draw two concentric circles directly below the origin, and two more at the lower-left and lower-right angles.
 
img width="203" height="260" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-16 014635" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/71af6595-48aa-4bac-950b-9b5560dba8a0" />
   
8. Connect the top arm: Draw two Tangent Arcs connecting the outer circle of the top ear smoothly to the sides of the center hub.

<img width="307" height="52" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-16 015805" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e02bb4e8-708a-4b7e-8dd0-e74fdf00d954" />

9. Connect the lower arms: Draw Tangent Arcs connecting the outer circles of the three bottom ears to the main body and to each other.
 
10. Clean up lines: Use the Trim tool to remove all overlapping inner construction lines so you have one closed profile.

 <img width="871" height="86" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-16 020243" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ed3b1471-80e1-4410-a108-f1c0fbf2a029" />

11. Extrude the part: Finish the sketch and use the Extrude tool to pull the flat profile into a 3D solid body.
